CropIt

Regenerative Insights for Real-World Profits 🌾🚀

inspiration.txt

> Inspiration

Our journey started with a love for food, climate action, and the powerful stories of Gabe Brown, Russell Hedrick, and real working farms. Watching Nature's Balance: The Art of Regenerative Farming and Chef’s Table, we saw firsthand that regenerative agriculture is not only possible, it’s the future economically and ecologically.

For years, climate targets have fallen short often because “sustainability” felt like a cost, not an opportunity. Cropit is built to flip that script by showing how regenerative agriculture can be a profitable path for farms and food businesses.

What really grabbed us:

  • ✧ Gabe Brown’s 6 Principles: context, least disturbance, soil armor, diversity, living root, animal integration—are real-world wisdom, not just theory.
  • ✧ Russell Hedrick’s data: over $62,000 saved in a year with numbers to prove it, not just hope!
  • ✧ The relentless challenge of monocropping: most US row crop farms plant just 2–3 crops, nowhere near the diversity science recommends.
  • ✧ Our team’s foodie spirit: believing every great meal starts with soil health.
what_it_does.md

> 🤖 What It Does

Cropit is your farm’s collaborative dashboard and “helping hand” for all things regenerative. Here’s the magic:

  • ✧ Integrates real-world data (USDA, OpenWeather, live web scraping via Gemini API, satellite sources) to power actionable farm analytics.
  • ✧ Tracks profit, diversity, and resilience for every field. See the “Eco-Wallet”: money saved, risks avoided, new revenue streams (like carbon credits).
  • ✧ Gives proactive insights how to boost soil health, manage crop rotations, and prepare for weather or market changes.
  • ✧ Visualizes advanced metrics: Fungal:Bacterial Ratio, real-time Brix (sap) readings, crop rotation diversity, water-holding capacity, and more.
  • ✧ Not just a dashboard. Cropit is a digital partner, helping you make informed, profitable, sustainable choices, not just handing down “advice.”
crisis_report.txt

> Problem: Farming at a Breaking Point

The Reality: American farmers are under serious pressure. Farm bankruptcy filings have been rising again, with hundreds of family farms seeking protection as input costs stay high and margins stay thin.

Surveys show a large share of farmers believe we are on the verge of a farming crisis. While regenerative practices can help long-term, they often require upfront costs and risks that farmers running on thin margins simply cannot absorb.

The Result: Farmers do the "rational" thing: they monocrop, chase short-term market signals, and hope to make it to next season.

> The Vision

CropIt starts from a simple idea: If predictive AI can show farmers when sustainability is also the most profitable option, change becomes a spreadsheet decision instead of a leap of faith.

We aim to help farmers:

  • Stay profitable in volatile markets.
  • Shift toward regenerative practices when numbers support the move.
  • Survive the current crisis without burning out their land.
design_manifesto.md

> Design Philosophy

"Designed less like a Silicon Valley finance tool and more like a calm copilot for a very tired person who has been awake since 4 a.m."

1. Farmer-First Language

We use terms like "fields," "pest risk," and "rainfall window," not abstract KPIs. The interface speaks in scenarios: "If you delay planting by a week, your risk changes like this."

2. Hands are Busy, Attention is Limited

Includes an audio briefing so farmers can listen while driving a tractor. Large 48-pixel touch targets are sized specifically for gloved hands.

3. Dark at 5 a.m., Blinding at 2 p.m.

A three-mode theme optimizes for pre-dawn cab lighting (soft charcoal to reduce eye strain) and bright outdoor use (high contrast).

4. Accessibility & Calm

Information is layered using progressive disclosure. The goal is to reduce the number of times a farmer has to ask, "What is this thing trying to tell me?"

ai_logic.log

> AI In The Loop

Not "Robot Farmer", More "Very Fast Intern"

AI in CropIt has one job: do math and paperwork faster so humans can make better decisions. It does not decide what a farm should become.

  • >> Predictive Analytics: Models yields and costs to compare "business as usual" vs. regenerative options.
  • >> Scenario Framing: Instead of commands, it offers context: "Here are three scenarios, one of which both pays your bills and helps your soil."
  • >> Bureaucracy Killer: AI scans and pre-fills routine compliance forms (drag-and-drop PDFs), flagging sensitive info for human review.

> Interface Structure

  • Dashboard: "Am I okay right now?" (Metric cards, yield forecast, sensor feed).
  • Fields Map: "Where is the problem?" (Highlights outliers like nitrogen stress).
  • Weather: "When should I act?" (Suggests practical windows for spraying/planting).
  • Sustainability: "Can I afford to be better?" (Side-by-side economic vs. ecological comparison).
how_we_built_it.log

> 🛠️ How We Built It

Stack: Next.js, Python, Gemini API (web scraping/insights), ElevenLabs (audio alerts), OpenWeather, USDA agricultural data, Amazon Nova (contextual AI insights), Knot (transaction analytics), Capital One API (financial tools).

UI/UX:

Crafted with Figma iterations, prioritizing clarity for high-stakes decisions.

Data scraping:

Leveraged Gemini’s API to pull fresh web/crop/soil/weather data.

Analytics:

Developed “Eco-Wallet” cost models leveraging real farm data calculations.

Voice & Context:

ElevenLabs reads alerts; Amazon Nova automates deep-dive research.

Financials: Knot's API tracks transactions, Capital One's API enables financial benchmarking.

challenges.log

> 🚧 Challenges We Ran Into

  • ✧ API integration: Combining many data streams (USDA, weather, web scraping) presented rate limits and inconsistent data schemas.
  • ✧ Data overload: Modern farming generates mountains of data. Our toughest task was making this simple and actionable.
  • ✧ Communicating complexity: Regenerative ag is science + creativity. Making advanced analytics enjoyable was a constant balance.
  • ✧ Climate variability: Farming data is at the mercy of the weather. We designed not for “average” but for the wild, unpredictable future.

> Iteration

Davina's CropIt Breakdown

My diagram visualizes the "CropIt" platform's theory of change, mapping the user journey from a "Current Crisis" of failing margins and short-term survivalism to a "Sustainable Future" of long-term profitability. It defines the platform's role not as a robotic replacement but as a predictive AI assistant, a "Very Fast Intern," that helps farmers overcome the dilemma of "Profit Today vs. Soil Tomorrow" by framing regenerative choices as calculated "spreadsheet decisions" rather than financial risks. The system architecture highlights a "Farmer-First" UX philosophy featuring accessible mobile interfaces with large touch targets and day/night modes, alongside a "Human-in-the-Loop" compliance workflow that automates bureaucracy while keeping the farmer in control. Ultimately, the schematic demonstrates how the software bridges the gap between immediate economic pressure and ecological resilience, enabling the user to achieve a state where they possess "profit and soil health together".

System Architecture Diagram

The system architecture diagram illustrates the comprehensive infrastructure of CropIt, showcasing the integration of various APIs and data sources that power the platform. Key components include data ingestion from USDA databases, real-time weather updates via OpenWeather API, and web scraping capabilities through Gemini API. The architecture also highlights the use of ElevenLabs for audio alerts and Amazon Nova for contextual AI insights. The front-end is built with Next.js, ensuring a responsive and user-friendly interface, while back-end processes handle data analytics and eco-wallet calculations. This diagram serves as a blueprint for understanding how CropIt seamlessly combines multiple technologies to deliver actionable insights for regenerative agriculture.

New Landing Page

The UI/UX strategy for the revamped CropIt landing page is grounded in User-Centered Design (UCD) principles which specifically target the reduction of cognitive load for agribusiness professionals facing high-stakes decisions. By adopting a dark-mode aesthetic with a minimalistic grid overlay, the interface minimizes visual noise to enhance data legibility and reduce eye strain which is a critical factor for users monitoring complex metrics over extended periods. The visual hierarchy is rigorously structured through scale and contrast by employing a prominent sans-serif typography for the value proposition to immediately establish semantic salience, while the strategic use of the color green serves as a signifier for both the sustainability domain and the primary Call-to-Action (CTA) to guide the user's focal point efficiently towards conversion. This iterative design evolution reflects a heuristic prioritization of clarity and efficiency that transforms complex agricultural intelligence into an accessible and intuitive digital experience aligned with the mental models of modern and data-driven farmers.

New Interface

The new interface design for CropIt emphasizes a clean, modern aesthetic with a focus on usability and accessibility. The layout is optimized for both desktop and mobile devices, ensuring seamless navigation across platforms. Key features include a responsive grid system, intuitive menu structures, and streamlined workflows that reduce user friction. The interface also incorporates subtle animations and transitions to enhance the user experience without overwhelming the core functionality.

Weather Page

The Weather Page within CropIt provides real-time meteorological data and forecasts tailored for agricultural decision-making from their location data. Users can access detailed weather reports, historical data visualizations, and customizable alerts to stay informed about critical weather events that may impact their crops.

Document Vault

The Document Vault within CropIt provides a centralized repository for all agricultural documentation, including field notes, crop management plans, and regulatory compliance records. Users can securely store, organize, and retrieve documents with advanced search capabilities and version control features.

Fields Page

The Fields Page within CropIt provides a comprehensive view of all agricultural fields, including detailed maps, soil analysis, and crop health indicators. Users can manage field-specific data, track planting schedules, and monitor growth patterns to optimize yield and resource allocation. There is also thermal health index visualization for better crop monitoring. We specifically chose the location of Salinas, California for the demo farm due to its reputation as the "Salad Bowl of the World," known for its intensive vegetable production and progressive farming practices, making it an ideal setting to showcase CropIt's capabilities in supporting sustainable agriculture. Furthermore, it is the closest farm to San Francisco, where we participated in the Dedalus Labs Break In Program.

AI Farm Assistant

The AI Farm Assistant within CropIt provides intelligent recommendations and insights for crop management, leveraging machine learning algorithms to analyze field data, weather patterns, and historical yields. Users can receive personalized advice on planting schedules, irrigation needs, and pest control strategies to maximize productivity.
